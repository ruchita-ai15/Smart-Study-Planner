How the Scheduling Algorithm Works
  • Difficulty Weighting: Hard topics (weight 3) are scheduled earlier than Medium (2) and Easy (1) to tackle complex concepts first.
  • Exam Urgency: Topics belonging to subjects with upcoming target exam dates receive higher priority.
  • Workload Balancing: Daily hours are strictly capped at your available hours to prevent burnout.
  • Deadline Protection: Topics are scheduled before their subject's exam date; warnings are displayed if additional daily study time is needed.
  • Completed Topics Excluded: Any topic already marked completed is automatically omitted from new schedules.
